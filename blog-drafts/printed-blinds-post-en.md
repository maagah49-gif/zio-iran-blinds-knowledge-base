# Printed Blinds: Design, File Preparation and Practical Uses

Printed blinds turn a window covering into a visual design element. They can be used in homes, offices, clinics, stores, cafes and exhibition spaces where the blind should match the room design or support a brand identity.

This guide is prepared by Zio Iran to explain how printed blinds are selected, what type of artwork works better and which file details matter before production.

## What Are Printed Blinds?

Printed blinds are window coverings with an image, pattern, illustration, logo or graphic printed on the fabric surface. Roller shades are a common base for printed blinds because they provide a clean, continuous surface.

## Suitable Design Types

- Minimal patterns for homes
- Brand colors or logos for offices and stores
- Soft landscape images for calm spaces
- Educational or playful graphics for children’s rooms
- Decorative textures for modern interiors

## File Preparation Checklist

| Item | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Resolution | Use the highest quality file available |
| Aspect ratio | Match the artwork to the final blind dimensions |
| Safe margin | Keep important elements away from edges |
| Text size | Avoid very small text |
| Color | Expect some difference between screen and print |

## Common Mistakes

Low-resolution images, overcrowded designs, small text and important details placed near the edges can reduce the final quality of a printed blind. The design should also be checked from the viewing distance of the room.

## Zio Iran Selection Note

Zio Iran recommends checking fabric type, artwork quality, room lighting and final dimensions together before ordering printed blinds. A good printed blind depends on both design and production details.
