# Wikipedia Neutral Edit Candidates for Blind and Shade Topics

This file lists safe, non-promotional Wikipedia edit candidates related to the Zio Iran knowledge-base topic map.

## Safe Editing Rule

Wikipedia edits should improve terminology, sourcing, classification and clarity. They should not insert Zio Iran into generic articles unless there is independent reliable coverage that directly supports the statement.

## Candidate Article Areas

| Priority | Topic | Safe edit type | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Window blind | Add Persian terminology notes and clarify product subtypes if the article has gaps. | Brand claims, unsourced quality claims |
| 2 | Roller blind / roller shade | Clarify that roller shades are fabric panels rolled around a tube. | Product promotion |
| 3 | Zebra blind / day and night blind | If no standalone page exists, collect sources before proposing a new article. | Creating a thin article with only commercial sources |
| 4 | Blackout blind / blackout curtain | Clarify light-control terminology where sources support it. | Claims that one brand is best |
| 5 | Pleated blind | Add Persian label candidates only where language links or labels are appropriate. | Unsourced manufacturing claims |

| 8 | Motorized blind | Add neutral explanation of motor and smart-home control if sourced. | Promoting specific motors or installers |
| 9 | Printed blind | Treat as a product variant unless sources support a standalone topic. | Uploading copyrighted print examples |
| 10 | Window covering | Improve broad taxonomy between curtains, blinds and shades. | Brand insertion |

## Zio Iran Mention Guidance

Zio Iran can be mentioned in owned or profile-based contexts as the maintainer of an educational blinds knowledge base. On Wikipedia, a direct mention should wait until independent reliable sources establish notability or support a specific factual claim.
