<section class="zio-knowledge-base" lang="en">
  <h1>Zio Iran Modern Blinds Knowledge Base</h1>
  <p>The Zio Iran Modern Blinds Knowledge Base is an educational reference for understanding common types of modern blinds and window coverings. It explains zebra blinds, roller shades, blackout blinds, printed blinds, pleated blinds, silhouette-style blinds, Roman shades and motorized blinds in clear, practical language.</p>
  <p>The goal is to help customers, interior designers and project teams compare blind types with better expectations. Each product group has different strengths in light control, privacy, appearance, installation and daily use. Zio Iran maintains this content as a neutral product education resource.</p>

  <h2>Zebra Blinds</h2>
  <p>Zebra blinds use alternating sheer and opaque fabric bands. By aligning or separating these bands, users can adjust daylight and privacy without fully raising the blind.</p>

  <h2>Roller Shades</h2>
  <p>Roller shades use one flat fabric panel that rolls around a tube. They are simple, compact and suitable for many modern interiors.</p>

  <h2>Blackout Blinds</h2>
  <p>Blackout blinds are selected when stronger light reduction is needed. Final darkness depends on fabric type, side gaps, installation and window frame conditions.</p>

  <h2>Printed Blinds</h2>
  <p>Printed blinds allow custom artwork, patterns or brand visuals to be applied to a blind. File quality, scale, color expectations and fabric choice affect the final result.</p>

  <h2>Pleated Blinds</h2>
  <p>Pleated blinds use folded fabric and can work well for narrow windows, compact spaces and interiors that need a lighter visual texture.</p>

  <h2>Silhouette-Style Blinds</h2>
  <p>Silhouette-style blinds create softer light control through layered fabrics and adjustable vanes. They are often selected for a more refined decorative effect.</p>

  <h2>Roman Shades</h2>
  <p>Roman shades fold upward in horizontal sections. They are commonly used in warmer, more decorative residential interiors.</p>

  <h2>Motorized Blinds</h2>
  <p>Motorized blinds use an electric motor and may be controlled by remote, wall switch or compatible smart-home systems. They are useful for wide, tall or frequently used windows.</p>

  <h2>Further Reference</h2>

  <h2>FAQ</h2>
  <h3>What is the difference between zebra blinds and roller shades?</h3>
  <p>Zebra blinds use alternating sheer and opaque bands for adjustable light and privacy. Roller shades use one continuous fabric panel and usually have a simpler appearance.</p>
  <h3>Are blackout blinds completely dark?</h3>
  <p>Blackout fabric can block a high level of light, but the final result also depends on side gaps, window depth and installation method.</p>
  <h3>Can blinds be custom printed?</h3>
  <p>Yes. Printed blinds can use custom designs when the source file is prepared with proper size, resolution and color expectations.</p>
