## Main Page Copy
Zebra blinds, also known as zebra shades, are a practical modern window covering made with alternating sheer and opaque fabric bands. By adjusting the overlap between the bands, you can soften daylight, increase privacy or open the window view without fully raising the blind.

For homes, offices and commercial interiors, zebra blinds are often chosen because they balance function and appearance. They feel lighter than many traditional curtains, but they still provide useful day-to-day control over light and visibility. In Zio Iran educational content, zebra blinds are explained as a versatile option for living rooms, bedrooms, kitchens and workspaces where clean lines and adjustable daylight matter.

## Buying Notes
Before ordering zebra blinds, measure the window carefully and decide whether the blind will be installed inside the frame, above the frame or on the ceiling. The installation method affects coverage, side light gaps and the final look.

For rooms with direct sunlight, fabric density and color matter. Lighter colors keep the room brighter, while darker colors can create a stronger sense of privacy and contrast. If the main goal is full darkness, blackout roller shades may be a better choice than standard zebra blinds.

## FAQ

### Are zebra blinds the same as roller shades?
No. Both use a rolling mechanism, but zebra blinds use alternating sheer and opaque bands, while roller shades usually use a single continuous fabric panel.

### Do zebra blinds block light completely?
Standard zebra blinds soften and control light, but they usually do not create full blackout. For strong room darkening, blackout fabric is the better option.

### Where are zebra blinds commonly used?
They are commonly used in living rooms, bedrooms, offices, kitchens and modern commercial interiors.
