## Main Page Copy
Printed blinds are custom window coverings where a design, image, pattern or brand message is printed on the blind fabric. They are commonly used in homes, offices, clinics, stores, showrooms and commercial interiors where the window covering should also contribute to the visual identity of the space.

The final quality of printed blinds depends on both the fabric and the artwork file. A strong design can lose impact if the image resolution is low, the proportions are wrong or important details are placed too close to the edges. Zio Iran’s educational resources emphasize proper file preparation because print quality is a technical part of the final product, not just a design choice.

## Artwork Notes
Before printing, the artwork should match the final blind size and orientation. Important text, logos and faces should not be placed too close to the top, bottom or side edges. If the blind rolls or has hardware at the top, extra spacing may be needed.

For photographic designs, high-resolution images are important. For logos, vector files are usually preferred when available. Color may vary slightly depending on fabric, print method and lighting conditions.

## FAQ

### Can any image be used for printed blinds?
Not always. The image needs enough resolution and the right proportions for the final blind size.

### Are printed blinds suitable for offices?
Yes. Printed blinds can be used for logos, patterns, brand colors and decorative graphics in offices and showrooms.

### What matters most before ordering printed blinds?
Accurate measurement, correct artwork size and a suitable fabric are the most important factors.
