## Main Page Copy
Roman shades are fabric window coverings that fold into horizontal sections when raised. They are often chosen by people who like the warmth of fabric curtains but prefer a cleaner and more structured form.

Because Roman shades depend heavily on fabric choice, they can feel classic, modern, soft or formal depending on the material and color. In Zio Iran’s educational guides, Roman shades are introduced as a bridge between traditional fabric curtains and modern blind systems.

## Why Choose Roman Shades?
- Warm fabric appearance
- Neat horizontal folds
- Suitable for bedrooms and living rooms
- Wide fabric and color possibilities
- Works with classic, modern and transitional interiors

## Buying Notes
Fabric selection is the most important part of ordering Roman shades. Lighter fabrics create a softer look, while heavier fabrics provide stronger coverage and a more formal feel. For bedrooms, lining or thicker fabric may be needed if privacy or darkness is important.

Roman shades can be installed inside or outside the window frame depending on the desired appearance and coverage. Measurement should account for folds, mounting space and hardware.

## FAQ

### Are Roman shades good for bedrooms?
Yes. They can work well in bedrooms, especially when a suitable fabric or lining is selected.

### Are Roman shades modern or classic?
They can be either. The fabric, color and styling determine whether the final look feels modern, classic or transitional.

### Are Roman shades easy to maintain?
Maintenance depends on fabric type and construction. Always check the fabric care instructions before cleaning.
