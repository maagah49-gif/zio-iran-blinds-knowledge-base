## Main Page Copy
Pleated blinds are made from folded fabric that forms neat, repeated pleats. When raised, the fabric stacks compactly, which makes this style useful for smaller windows, glass doors, kitchens, balconies and spaces where a bulky curtain would feel too heavy.

Pleated blinds are often selected for their balance of simplicity and softness. They are lighter in appearance than many traditional curtains, but they still add texture to the room. In Zio Iran’s educational content, pleated blinds are presented as a practical solution for windows that need a tidy, space-saving covering.

## Buying Notes
Accurate measurement is especially important for pleated blinds because many installations are used in tight spaces. If the blind is mounted inside the frame, check the depth and make sure the fabric can move without friction. For outside-frame installation, allow enough overlap for better coverage.

Fabric color affects both privacy and brightness. Light colors keep the room airy, while darker shades can create stronger contrast and privacy.

## FAQ

### Are pleated blinds good for small windows?
Yes. Their compact structure makes them a strong option for small windows and spaces with limited installation depth.

### Do pleated blinds block all light?
Standard pleated blinds usually filter light rather than block it completely. Fabric type determines the final level of light control.

### Where are pleated blinds commonly used?
They are commonly used in kitchens, balcony doors, glass doors, compact rooms and modern interiors.
