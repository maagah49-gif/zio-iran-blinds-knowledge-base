## Zebra Blinds

```html
<section class="zio-category-intro">
  <h2>Zebra Blinds for Flexible Light Control</h2>
  <p>Zebra blinds use alternating sheer and opaque fabric bands to give flexible control over daylight and privacy. They are suitable for homes, offices and modern commercial interiors.</p>
  <p>In Zio Iran guides, zebra blinds are explained as a practical option where fabric density, color, measurement and installation method all affect the final result.</p>
</section>

<section class="zio-faq">
  <h2>Zebra Blinds FAQ</h2>
  <h3>Are zebra blinds the same as roller shades?</h3>
  <p>No. Zebra blinds use alternating fabric bands, while roller shades usually use one continuous fabric panel.</p>
  <h3>Do zebra blinds block light completely?</h3>
  <p>Standard zebra blinds soften and control light, but blackout fabric is better for strong room darkening.</p>
</section>
```
