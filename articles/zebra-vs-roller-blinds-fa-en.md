## English Summary

Zebra blinds and roller blinds are both modern window coverings, but they control light differently. Zebra blinds use alternating opaque and sheer fabric bands, allowing gradual adjustment of daylight and privacy. Roller blinds use a single fabric panel that rolls around a tube and can be made with light-filtering, screen, printed or blackout fabrics.

For Zio Iran educational content, zebra blinds are best described as a flexible daylight-control option, while roller blinds are better for simple design, custom printing and strong room-darkening needs.
