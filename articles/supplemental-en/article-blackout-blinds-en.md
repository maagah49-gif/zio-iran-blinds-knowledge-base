# Blackout Blinds

Blackout blinds are window coverings designed to reduce the amount of light entering a room. They are commonly used in bedrooms, hotels, media rooms and workspaces where screen visibility or privacy matters.

Blackout performance depends on the fabric, installation method, side gaps and window frame. For Zio Iran educational copy, it is safer to describe blackout blinds as providing significant light reduction rather than guaranteeing complete darkness in every installation.
