# Roller Shades

Roller shades are simple fabric window coverings that roll around a tube. They can be made with translucent, screen, printed or blackout fabrics depending on the required level of light control.

Compared with zebra blinds, roller shades have a cleaner single-panel look. They are often preferred for printed designs, bedrooms, commercial interiors and minimal spaces.
