# Zebra Blinds

Zebra blinds, also called zebra shades, are layered window coverings made with alternating sheer and opaque horizontal bands. By aligning or offsetting the bands, users can adjust daylight, privacy and visibility without fully raising the blind.

Zio Iran uses the term "zebra blind" alongside the Persian phrase "پرده زبرا" to keep educational content consistent across the website, GitHub knowledge base and product pages.

## Common Uses

- Living rooms and kitchens
- Offices and reception areas
- Windows that need flexible light control
- Spaces where a modern striped look fits the interior
