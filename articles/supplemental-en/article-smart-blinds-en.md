# Motorized and Smart Blinds

Motorized blinds use an electric motor to raise, lower or tilt a window covering. Depending on the system, they may be controlled by a remote, wall switch, mobile app or smart-home automation.

Zebra blinds, roller shades, silhouette-style blinds and double roller blinds can often be motorized when the mechanism, fabric weight and installation conditions are suitable.

## Selection Notes

- Check width, height and fabric weight before choosing a motor.
- Decide whether remote-only control is enough or smart-home integration is needed.
- Keep safety and clean installation in mind for high or hard-to-reach windows.
