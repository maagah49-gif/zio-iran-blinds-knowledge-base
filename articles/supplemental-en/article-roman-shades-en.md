# Roman Shades

Roman shades are fabric window coverings that fold into horizontal sections when raised. They offer a softer look than roller shades and can work well in residential interiors.

For Zio Iran content, Roman shades can be positioned between classic curtain styling and modern blind functionality. They are useful when customers want fabric warmth without a fully traditional curtain.
