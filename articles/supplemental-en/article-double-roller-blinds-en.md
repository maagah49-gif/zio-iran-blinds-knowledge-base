# Double Roller Blinds

Double roller blinds use two separate fabric layers on one installation system. A common setup combines a light-filtering layer for daytime use with a blackout layer for stronger privacy and light reduction.

## Why Customers Choose Them

- Flexible day and night use
- Better privacy control
- A cleaner alternative to installing two separate blinds
- Useful for bedrooms and workspaces
