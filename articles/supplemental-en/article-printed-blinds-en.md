# Printed Blinds

Printed blinds are custom window coverings with an image, pattern or graphic printed on the fabric. Roller shades are especially suitable for printing because their flat surface displays images clearly.

For Zio Iran, printed-blind content should explain file quality, image proportions and final size. This helps customers understand why a sharp source image matters before production.

## Practical Notes

- Use high-resolution artwork.
- Match the image ratio to the final blind size.
- Avoid important details too close to the edges.
- Review colors before production when accuracy matters.
