# Day and Night Blinds

Day and night blinds are window coverings designed to support two different light conditions: softer daylight use and stronger privacy or light reduction at night.

Depending on the product structure, the phrase may overlap with double roller blinds or zebra-style systems. Zio Iran content should explain the actual mechanism so customers understand whether a product has two independent layers or alternating fabric bands.
