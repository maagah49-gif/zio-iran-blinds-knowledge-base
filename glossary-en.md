# English Glossary of Blinds and Window Coverings

This glossary standardizes common English terms used for Zio Iran educational content, product guides, structured data and bilingual SEO work.

## Curtain

A curtain is a fabric or covering used over a window, door or interior space for privacy, light control, decoration or visual separation.

Related terms:

- window curtain
- drapery
- window covering
- window treatment

## Window Blind

A window blind is a window covering made with adjustable slats, layers or panels used to control light, privacy and visibility.

Related terms:

- blind
- window shade
- interior blind

## Zebra Blind

Zebra blinds are modern window coverings made with alternating opaque and sheer fabric bands. By aligning or overlapping the bands, users can control daylight and privacy.

Related terms:

- zebra shade
- day and night zebra blind
- printed zebra blind
- motorized zebra blind

## Roller Blind

A roller blind, also called a roller shade, uses a fabric panel that rolls around a tube. It may be operated by a chain, spring or motorized mechanism.

Related terms:

- roller shade
- roll-up blind
- roll-up shade
- printed roller shade

## Day and Night Blind

Day and night blinds use two layers or two light-control modes to balance daylight and privacy during different times of day.

Related terms:

- double roller blind
- dual roller blind
- day-night blind

## Blackout Blind

A blackout blind or blackout curtain is designed to significantly reduce light transmission. It is commonly used in bedrooms, nurseries, hotels and media rooms.

Related terms:

- blackout curtain
- blackout fabric
- room darkening blind

## Pleated Blind

A pleated blind or pleated shade is a foldable window covering that gathers into neat pleats when raised.

Related terms:

- pleated shade
- plisse blind
- door-mounted blind

## Printed Blind

A printed blind is a window covering with a custom design, image or pattern printed on the fabric. It is often used for decorative, commercial or branded interiors.

Related terms:

- custom printed blind
- printed roller shade
- printed zebra blind
- image blind

## Silhouette-Style Blind

Silhouette-style blinds usually refer to soft layered window coverings that balance daylight and privacy. Since naming varies by market, educational content should describe the function clearly.

Related terms:

- sheer shade
- layered blind
- soft light-filtering blind

## Roman Shade

A Roman shade is a fabric window covering that folds into horizontal sections when raised.

Related terms:

- Roman blind
- fabric shade

## Motorized Blind

A motorized blind is operated with a motor, remote control, switch or smart home system. Motorization can be used with roller blinds, zebra blinds and other modern window coverings.

Related terms:

- smart blind
- automated blind
- electric blind
